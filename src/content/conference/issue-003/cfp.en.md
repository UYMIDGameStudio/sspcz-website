The 3rd SSPCZ invites paper submissions from secondary school students passionate about philosophy. Selection is based on overall merit: after submissions close, papers are ranked by final review score to determine presenting and attending papers. There is no fixed quota for each recommended direction; numbers will be announced once venue and programme arrangements are confirmed.

## Eligibility

Secondary school students with a deep interest in philosophy who wish to present their research. Submissions must include an abstract, the full paper and a signed [Academic and Ethics Declaration](/documents/academic-ethics-declaration.docx). The declaration is currently available in Chinese.

## Theme

Topics should engage the theme "Change & Invariance": in a life-world of constant flux, what constitutes the invariant we ought to hold — and how can that holding stay alive within change? All serious philosophical work is welcome. The full exposition is in the committee's letter.

## Review Criteria (50 points total)

1. Argumentation (persuasiveness, logic and internally coherent citation) — 12 points
2. Theory, including fit with the selected topic (alignment with the theme and scholarly value) — 12 points
3. Originality (novelty and independent contribution) — 10 points
4. Use of concepts — 6 points
5. Academic standards (citation: 4 points; formatting: 2 points) — 6 points
6. Language and expression — 4 points

## Awards

Four prizes are conferred: Best Writing, Best Presentation, Best Theme and Best Originality. One author may win multiple awards.

## Originality

Papers must be original, unpublished in any form and not under review elsewhere; they must pass academic-integrity screening, with authorship based on actual contribution.

## AI Policy

AI may not be an author or co-author, and no more than 15% of the main text may be AI-generated. Authors who use AI must disclose the prompts, tools and versions in all three places: the cover letter, acknowledgements and methods section. Authors who did not use AI need only submit a declaration of non-use.

## Deadline and Submission

The submission deadline is 20 September 2026 (Sunday), 23:59, subject to formal notice. Papers submitted on or before 15 September may enter revision and re-review if changes are requested. Papers submitted from 16 September onward have no revision stage, and the review decision takes immediate effect.

For submissions and partnership, contact the committee by email with your abstract, full paper and signed Academic and Ethics Declaration.

> Detailed rules follow the official document issued by the committee's Academic Team; full review and integrity requirements are in the Conference Policies.
